# Support hours and escalation

Standard support is available Monday-Friday, 9am-6pm IST via the merchant
dashboard's ticketing system. Payment-down or settlement-blocking issues are
treated as priority regardless of the hour and should be escalated
immediately rather than queued for the next business day.

Anything outside payments, refunds, reconciliation, or fraud — account
access, KYC/onboarding document requests, API key regeneration, dashboard
user-permission changes — should route to a human agent rather than being
answered generically, since those require identity verification this
assistant cannot perform.

## Invoices and tax documents

Monthly invoices for platform fees and the corresponding GST documents are
generated automatically and available under the dashboard's Reports section
by the 5th business day of the following month; they are not emailed
proactively unless the merchant has opted into that notification.

## Escalation matrix

- Payment/refund/settlement questions with a specific ID → handled directly
  by the specialist agents.
- Account, KYC, or access issues → human support, priority queue.
- Suspected fraud or unauthorized account access → escalate immediately to
  the risk team, do not attempt to resolve via standard support.
- Anything unclear or spanning multiple categories → default to a human
  rather than guessing, since payments support carries real financial impact.
